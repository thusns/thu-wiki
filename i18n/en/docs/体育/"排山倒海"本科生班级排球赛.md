# "Paishandaohai 排山倒海" Undergraduate Class Volleyball Tournament

## Organizing Committee

Jointly organized by the Culture and Sports Department of the Communist Youth League Committee of Tsinghua University (共青团清华大学委员会文体部) and the Sports Department of the Tsinghua University Student Union (清华大学学生会体育部). Responsible for all preparations and organization of this volleyball tournament.

## Arbitration Committee

Jointly organized by the Culture and Sports Department of the Communist Youth League Committee of Tsinghua University (共青团清华大学委员会文体部) and the Sports Department of the Tsinghua University Student Union (清华大学学生会体育部). Responsible for arbitration of this volleyball tournament.

## Schedule and Competition Format

### Competition Format

- Phase 1: **Preliminary Round (预选赛)**
- Phase 2: **Wildcard Round (外卡赛)**
- Phase 3: **Main Tournament (正式赛)**

#### Preliminary Round

The intra-departmental volleyball league of alliance member departments. The competition format is determined by **each department**. The **1st place** finisher in each department's preliminary round directly qualifies for the **Main Tournament** in the spring semester.

#### Wildcard Round

Any undergraduate team that did not qualify for the Main Tournament through the Preliminary Round may enter the Wildcard Round. Freshmen and senior students compete in **separate brackets** using a **single-elimination format**. The number of advancement slots for each bracket is determined by class registration numbers. The Wildcard Round determines a total of **64-X** Main Tournament slots (where X is the number of teams that directly qualified), with specific arrangements to be announced based on registration numbers.

#### Main Tournament

A total of **64** teams enter the Main Tournament. The tournament is divided into a group stage and a knockout stage, conducted in two phases.

##### Group Stage

Round-robin format: 64 teams are divided into 16 groups of 4 teams each for a round-robin. The top two teams in each group advance to the knockout stage. (Group rankings are first determined by number of wins. If two teams are tied, head-to-head record determines the ranking. If three teams are tied, average point differential among those three teams is used — excluding matches won by opponent forfeiture — to determine rankings. If average point differential is also tied, total points scored among those teams determines ranking highest to lowest. If still tied, a draw determines the rankings.)

##### Single Elimination

Continues until the champion is determined.

### Match Time and Venue

#### Match Time

- Departmental Preliminary Round: arranged by each department;
- Wildcard Round: Fall semester, specific times TBA;
- Main Tournament: Spring semester, specific times TBA.

#### Match Venue

All matches are held at the **East Sports Field Basketball Courts (东操篮球场)**, using courts 1, 2, 4, and 5. Courts are reserved by Culture and Sports Department staff before matches. If four matches start simultaneously, courts are assigned sequentially as 1, 2, 4, 5 according to the schedule. Courts 3 and 6 are reserved as backup and may be used if temporary court changes are needed.

#### Conditions for Requesting a Temporary Match Time Change

Coordination is conducted privately between teams; the Organizing Committee only verifies the schedule coordination. The Committee will determine whether to approve a time change based on factors such as impact on the tournament schedule. An email confirmation from the Committee constitutes approval.

##### Match Time Swap

If a team cannot field 5 starters at the designated time and place due to participation in university or departmental events, or due to Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) or Sports League events (non-personal reasons), they may negotiate in advance with their opponent and two other teams playing at another time slot. With the agreement of all 4 teams, and within the swap request deadline, a request email should be sent to the Committee email thuxhty@163.com, CC'd to the other 3 teams, with a reminder to Committee members in the team captains' group chat. The swap is confirmed upon Committee approval.

##### Swap Request Deadline

Any match time swap request must be submitted **before 10:00 PM on the Wednesday of the week of the originally scheduled match** (when matches are scheduled for Saturday/Sunday). Late requests will not be accepted.

##### Available Swap Times

Only the following Saturday/Sunday slots are available: 9:00-10:30, 10:30-12:00, 13:30-15:00, 15:00-16:30. In exceptional circumstances requiring a different time slot (e.g., a weekday or non-standard weekend time), an email explaining the reason must be sent in advance. Upon approval, the captain must reserve the court and contact a referee and match monitor (contact information provided by the Culture and Sports Department). The swap is official only if the referee and monitor agree; otherwise, the team that first requested the swap is considered to have forfeited.

## Eligibility and Registration

### Registration Unit

Any registered undergraduate class at Tsinghua University (清华大学) may register as an **administrative class** unit. Each administrative class may register **only 1 team**, with a roster of **at least 5 and at most 12 players**.

### Registration Eligibility

Each undergraduate department (or academy) at Tsinghua University may nominate 1 class team to enter the Main Tournament directly. Other teams from the department register collectively for the Wildcard Round to compete for Main Tournament slots. Two classes with fewer than 8 male students each (not including 8), from the same department and same year, may form a combined team. Combined teams are treated as a single class team and are scored equivalently.

### Player Eligibility

1. Registered players must be undergraduates officially enrolled in their class for the current academic year at Tsinghua University;
2. There is no gender restriction for players;
3. International students and exchange students may participate;
4. Targeted-admission students may only represent their administrative class and must provide certification from their department's Youth League/Work Committee;
5. Students admitted under Category A sports representative team standards are not eligible;
6. Students admitted under Category B sports representative team standards: For all matches except the semi-finals and finals of the Main Tournament, no more than 1 such student may be on the court at any time; each may play no more than 2 quarters, and total appearances by such students may not exceed 2 quarters. For semi-finals and finals, no more than 1 may be on the court at any time; each may play no more than 3 quarters, and total appearances may not exceed 3 quarters.
7. If administrative class changes occur during the season (e.g., due to major redistribution), players must continue to represent the team they were on before the change;
8. The Organizing Committee reserves the right to disqualify any team or combined team that does not meet the above eligibility requirements.

### Registration Method

#### Departmental Preliminary Round

Each department sets its own rules, organizes registration, and conducts the competition. Departments nominate Main Tournament teams based on their previous year's results.

#### Wildcard Round

Voluntary registration by class. There is no limit on the number of classes a department may register. Each class must have at least 5 and at most 12 players. Each team should also register 2 match monitors (who may be substitutes or other classmates). A match monitor training session will be held subsequently. The rules and registration materials will be sent to each department's Sports League liaison (typically the department student union president or the vice president in charge of sports). Teams should rename their registration form and submit it to the department liaison. The liaison should compile all registration forms and submit them as instructed.

## Dress Code

Each team must wear uniforms of a unified color scheme. If both teams wear the same color, the away team (listed on the right side in the schedule) must wear numbered jerseys.
