# "Linmen Yijiao 临门一脚" Undergraduate Class Football Tournament

## Organizing Committee

An Organizing Committee is established to prepare and organize this tournament. The Organizing Committee is composed of **representatives from the Culture and Sports Department of the Communist Youth League Committee of Tsinghua University (共青团清华大学委员会文体部)**.

## Competition Arbitration

Arbitration work shall follow the procedures outlined in Section IX "Hosting Details", Subsection (5) "Arbitration and Appeals" of the _2022-2023 Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) Competition Guide_.

## Schedule and Competition Format

### Competition Format

- Phase 1: **Senior Student Wildcard Round (老生区外卡赛)**
- Phase 2: **Zonal Triangular Round (分区三角赛)**
- Phase 3: **Mixed Triangular Round (混合三角赛)**
- Phase 4: **Knockout Round (淘汰赛)**

#### Senior Student Wildcard Round

A total of 72 team slots are allocated for the Senior Student Zone: 36 fixed slots (see Section V "Eligibility and Registration" for details), and 36 additional slots available through the wildcard round for classes that did not receive a departmental fixed slot. The wildcard round uses a **triangular format**, where teams are divided into groups of three by **drawing lots**, and each pair within a group plays a match. Each match lasts 20 minutes with no halftime break. Win = 3 points, draw = 1 point, loss = 0 points. The top 36 teams by total points advance to the next phase.

#### Zonal Triangular Round

During this phase, teams are divided into the **Senior Student Zone** and the **Freshman Zone** for separate competition. The Senior Student Zone triangular round consists of 72 teams, divided into groups of three by **drawing lots**, with each pair within a group playing a match. Each match lasts 20 minutes with no halftime break. Win = 3 points, draw = 1 point, loss = 0 points. The top 36 teams by total points advance to the next phase. The Freshman Zone triangular round follows the same format based on the number of registered teams, with the top 24 teams advancing.

#### Mixed Triangular Round

The Mixed Triangular Round has two stages: 60-to-36 and 36-to-16. In the 60-to-36 stage, the 36 advancing teams from the Senior Student Zone and the 24 advancing teams from the Freshman Zone are combined and divided into groups of three by **drawing lots**, with each pair within a group playing a match. Each match lasts 20 minutes with no halftime break. Win = 3 points, draw = 1 point, loss = 0 points. The top 36 teams advance. In the 36-to-16 stage, the same format applies, with the top 16 teams advancing.

##### Knockout Round

Based on scores from Phase 3, the top 8 teams are designated A1–A8, and the remaining 8 are designated B1–B8. The bracket is as follows:

Each knockout match lasts 30 minutes, split into two halves with a 5-minute halftime break. If the match ends in a draw, a penalty shootout determines the winner.

## Eligibility and Registration

### Eligibility

1. Any registered undergraduate class at Tsinghua University (清华大学) may register as an administrative class unit. Each team must have a minimum of 5 and a maximum of 12 players on the roster.
2. Each team may include up to 1 guest player, who must be a registered undergraduate in the same department/school and whose own class has not registered for this tournament. Guest players cannot be football specialists. Guest players must be noted as "guest player" in the "Remarks" column of the registration form.
3. Each undergraduate student may only represent 1 team. During the season, if a player's administrative class changes due to major redistribution or other special circumstances, the registration information at the time of sign-up shall prevail. Once registered, no student may represent another team.
4. Targeted-admission students may only represent their administrative class and must provide certification from their department's Youth League/Work Committee.
5. If two classes within the same department each have no more than 10 male students and belong to the same competition zone, they may form a combined team and register as a single class. Combined teams follow the same guest player rules as other teams.
6. Each team may include up to 1 football specialist, who must be noted as "football specialist" in the registration form.
7. There is no gender restriction for players.
8. The Organizing Committee reserves the right to disqualify any team or combined team that does not meet the above requirements.

### Quota Allocation

1. This tournament is divided into a Freshman Zone and a Senior Student Zone, with separate registration for each. The Freshman Zone is open to all 2022-cohort undergraduate administrative classes, with no limit on the number of registrations.
2. The Senior Student Zone follows fixed quotas assigned by the Organizing Committee. Each of the 36 departments with senior classes receives 1 fixed slot (see Appendix 1 for the list). Senior classes without a fixed departmental slot may register for the wildcard round. If a department does not fill its fixed slot, a deduction will be applied to the department's points, and the unused slots will be released to the wildcard round.

### Player Requirements

1. Player count: Each team must have at least 5 players. There is no limit on the number of substitutions per match. Substitutions must be reported to the referee for approval.
2. Attire: Players may wear turf football shoes, subject to the same requirements as the 2022-2023 Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) Men's Football Tournament regulations. If players wear jerseys, their numbers must match the registration information. If no jersey is available, the organizer will provide numbered bibs, and players must clearly identify their numbers.
3. Other equipment: Each team must bring a regulation size 5 football. If a special match ball is designated for the final, it will be announced in the following semester.

### Registration Method

Each team should fill out the registration form, list the players according to eligibility requirements, rename the file as "Football - Team Type - XX Department - XX Class" and submit it to the department coordinator.

Department coordinators should compile and package the forms, then send them to the Youth League Culture and Sports Department email: **_wenti@mail.tsinghua.edu.cn_** by 10:00 PM on October 13th. The paper versions, reviewed and stamped by the department's Youth League/Work Committee, should be submitted to Room 210, Building C by 5:00 PM on October 14th. Note: if the electronic registration is submitted multiple times, the last version before the deadline shall prevail.

At the beginning of the spring semester, all advancing teams will have one opportunity to adjust their rosters. Details will be communicated separately in the spring semester.

## Competition Rules

### General Rules

- During the triangular round stages, each match lasts 20 minutes with no halftime break and no stoppage time.
- During the knockout stage: each match lasts 30 minutes with two 15-minute halves and a 5-minute halftime break. If the match ends in a draw, a penalty shootout determines the winner.
  If one team forfeits, the other team is recorded as winning 5:0. If the actual score exceeds 5:0, the actual result stands. If both teams forfeit, both are recorded as losing 0:5.
  All other rules follow the Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) five-a-side football rules.

### Special Rules

1. In the first three triangular round stages, advancement is determined by point totals; if teams are tied on points, goal difference is compared; if still tied, goals scored are compared; if all three metrics are identical, a playoff is organized.
2. In the first and second triangular round stages, if the number of registered teams is not divisible by three, a special group of four teams is created during the draw, using a round-robin format. At the end of each stage, the results involving the last-placed team in this special group are removed (including points, goal difference, and goals scored), and the top three teams are incorporated into the overall rankings. The last-placed team in the special group is determined by points, head-to-head record, goal difference, and goals scored, in that order. If multiple teams are tied on all four criteria and the last place is at stake, a playoff is held.

## Competition Discipline

All teams must participate at the designated time and place. Except for weather, venue, or other objective factors, any team that fails to appear within 10 minutes of the scheduled start time will be considered to have forfeited. If a team cannot participate due to a major university or departmental event, they must apply to the Organizing Committee at least three days before the match and coordinate the match time with the other teams in their group.

If any team is found to have committed violations such as using substitute players in breach of the competition rules, their class will be immediately disqualified. The committee will handle other serious incidents on a case-by-case basis.

## Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) Points

Classes that complete the required schedule of the "Linmen Yijiao 临门一脚" Undergraduate Class Football Tournament will earn Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) points for their department. The maximum for this event is 60 points, split equally between base points and bonus points (30 each).

### Base Points (30 points)

#### Fixed Quota

Each department must complete registration according to the fixed quota allocation. If the quota is not filled, 30 base points will be deducted. See the Appendix for the list of departments with fixed quotas.

#### Match Participation

All departmental teams must complete all scheduled matches. If both teams agree to cancel a match, they must notify the organizer at least one day in advance. With the organizer's approval, no penalty will be applied; otherwise, 5 points per missed match will be deducted from the base points.

#### Scoring Details

Deductions are capped at the 30-point base total. If a department completes registration and all its teams complete all scheduled matches, the department earns the full 30 base points.

### Bonus Points

All teams that advance to the Round of 36 will earn bonus points based on their final ranking. The cumulative bonus points for each department are capped at 30. The correspondence between ranking and bonus points is as follows:

| Ranking      | Champion | Runner-up | Top 4 | Top 8 | Top 16 | Top 32 |
| ------------ | -------- | --------- | ----- | ----- | ------ | ------ |
| Bonus Points | 15       | 11        | 9     | 7     | 5      | 3      |
