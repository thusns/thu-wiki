# LaTeX and Other Templates

PPT template: https://github.com/atomiechen/THU-PPT-Theme

All templates below can be found and used at https://overleaf.tsinghua.edu.cn/templates/.

## "How to Typeset Papers with LaTeX" Lecture Notes

Since 2015, every April and November, the Tsinghua University Library hosts a special training lecture titled "How to Typeset Papers with LaTeX."

The project is available [here](https://github.com/alick/thulib-latex-talk), and a more recent fork is [here](https://github.com/tuna/thulib-latex-talk).

## ThuThesis

ThuThesis stands for Tsinghua University Thesis LaTeX Template.

This package aims to provide a simple and easy-to-use LaTeX template for Tsinghua University theses, including undergraduate comprehensive thesis training, master's theses, doctoral dissertations, and postdoctoral reports.

The project is available [here](https://github.com/tuna/thuthesis).

## ThuWordThesis

Tsinghua undergraduate thesis Word template.
