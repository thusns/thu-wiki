# Volunteer related information
## Volunteer resources
Most of the on-campus volunteer information will be sent out on the WeChat public account **"Tsinghua University Student Charity"**. The volunteer groups of each department will also carry out their own volunteer services, except for brand projects, most of which are internal to the department.
## Star Volunteer Selection
The selection of Tsinghua star volunteers is held around May every year. The selection is based on the number of volunteer hours and projects on [志愿北京网站](http://www.bv2008.cn), and the volunteers are ranked from one-star to five-star from low to high. The specific rules are as follows (excerpted from the "Tsinghua University Bauhinia Volunteer Graded Recognition Measures"):
* Log in to the Volunteer Beijing platform to register and fill in your personal information to register as a volunteer and join the volunteer group "Tsinghua University Bauhinia Volunteer Service Team". After participating in at least **one** volunteer service and obtaining certification time, you will be awarded the title of **"One-Star Bauhinia Volunteer"**.
* The cumulative volunteer service certification time reached **20** hours, and he was awarded the title of **"Two-Star Bauhinia Volunteer"**.
* The accumulated volunteer service certification time reached **50** hours and won the title of **"Three-Star Bauhinia Volunteer"**.
* The volunteer service certification time has reached **120** hours in total, and has participated in at least **3** different volunteer projects. After review, he was awarded the title of **"Four-Star Bauhinia Volunteer"**.
* The volunteer service certification time has reached **200** hours in total, and has participated in at least **5** different volunteer projects. After review, he was awarded the title of **"Five-Star Bauhinia Volunteer"**.
In addition, in the annual scholarship evaluation, there will also be an "Excellent Volunteer Scholarship" to reward students who have outstanding performance in volunteer services.
