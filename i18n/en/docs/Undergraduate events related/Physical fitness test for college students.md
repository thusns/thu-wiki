# Physical fitness test for college students

## Overview

There is a physical test every fall semester. First-year students take a centralized physical test (height, weight, and vital capacity will also be measured during the physical test). Sophomore and junior students are divided into two parts: a centralized physical test item and other items (height, weight, and vital capacity).

## Place

The centralized physical examination is generally entered from the northeast corner of Dongcao. After conducting preparatory activities, it can be carried out according to the instructions of the on-site staff.

Other physical examination items are conducted at the Taipei Point Physical Examination Center in Dongcao Xikan.

## Physical test guide

Just bring your student card. There will be boiled water, glucose water, etc. on site.

There is a big difference between testing and daily exercise. People with respiratory sensitivities may feel uncomfortable after finishing a mid-range run. They can wear a mask after running to relieve it.
