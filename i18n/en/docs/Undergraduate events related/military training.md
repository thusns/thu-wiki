# military training

### military skills

1. **Stand at attention, take a rest, turn, sit down, squat, stand up, walk in step, walk in step**
   (Turns during running and traveling were not examined)
2. **Military Boxing**
3. **Split-step** (that is, in front of the rostrum of Dongcao, it changes from marching to goose-stepping, and then to marching in tandem)
4. **Reporting performance**: Military boxing, assassination drill, flag language drill, dagger drill (each battalion selects one of the exercises)
5. **Shooting** (not live ammunition in recent years)
6. **Practice** (20 kilometers)

Score: 3.6 for most, 4.0 for outstanding performance
