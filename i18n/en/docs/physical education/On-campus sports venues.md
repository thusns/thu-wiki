# On-Campus Sports Venues

## Zijing Air Dome (紫荆气膜馆)
Location: North side of Zijing (紫荆) Building 18
Activities: Badminton, Table Tennis
Reservation: http://50.tsinghua.edu.cn

## Comprehensive Gymnasium (综合体育馆)
Location: Northeast of Teaching Building 6 (六教)
Activities: Basketball, Badminton
Reservation: https://50.tsinghua.edu.cn

## West Gymnasium (西体育馆)
Location: West of the West Sports Field (西操)
Activities: Badminton, Billiards
Reservation: https://50.tsinghua.edu.cn

## Zijing Tennis Courts (紫荆网球场)
Location: Northeast of Zijing Sports Field (紫操)
Activities: Tennis
Reservation: https://50.tsinghua.edu.cn

## Zijing Basketball Courts (紫荆篮球场)
Location: Southeast of Zijing Sports Field (紫操)
Activities: Basketball
Hours: Open all day except during class hours; nighttime lighting until 22:30 (summer schedule)
Reservation: Free; department representatives submit a court reservation slip on-site

## East Sports Field Basketball Courts (东操篮球场)
Location: East of Building 11 (11号楼)
Activities: Basketball
Hours: Open all day except during class hours; nighttime lighting until 22:00
Reservation: Free; department representatives submit a court reservation slip on-site

## West Sports Field Basketball Courts (西操篮球场)
Location: Northwest of the West Sports Field (西操)
Activities: Basketball
Hours: Open all day except during class hours; no nighttime lighting
Reservation: Free; department representatives submit a court reservation slip on-site

## East Sports Field Volleyball Courts (东操排球场)
Location: East of Building 12 (12号楼)
Activities: Volleyball
Hours: Open all day except during class hours; nighttime lighting until 22:00
Reservation: Free; department representatives submit a court reservation slip on-site

## Zijing Tennis Wall (紫操网球墙)
Location: Southeast of Zijing Sports Field (紫操), toward the courier pickup point
Activities: Tennis
Hours: Open all day
Reservation: Free, open access

## North Area Baseball Field (北区棒球场)
Location: Northeast of Chen Ming Swimming Center (陈明游泳馆)
Activities: Baseball
Hours: Open all day except during class hours; no nighttime lighting
Reservation: Free, open access

## Roller Skating Rink (轮滑场)
Location: East of the New Civil Engineering Building (新土木馆), west of the Comprehensive Gymnasium (综体)
Activities: Roller Skating
Hours: Open all day except during class hours; lights on Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday evenings 20:00–22:00
Reservation: Free, open access
