## Campus Services
**清华家园网 (Tsinghua Jiayuan)**: Provides student community-related services, including electricity bill payment, hygiene score inquiry, feedback, and seminar room reservations at Tsinghua Xuetang (清华学堂), Law Library (法图), Building 6 (六教), and Building 4 (四教).  
**学生清华 (Student Tsinghua)**: Provides room booking services for Building C 2F, Building C 3F, and the South Campus Underground Activity Room, as well as access to Shuimu Hui (水木汇) and the Freshman Handbook.  
**清华大学医院 (THU Hospital)**: Campus hospital WeChat account, providing registration and payment services. (Dental services require registration via the **清华后勤 (THU Logistics)** account);  
**清华后勤 (THU Logistics)**: Provides registration, repair requests, inquiry services, and more;  
**清华小清心 (THU Xiao Qingxin)**: Official WeChat account of the Psychological Development Guidance Center, offering counseling appointments, group counseling reservations, and more;  
**清华大学图书馆 (THU Library)**: Provides library seat reservations, opening/closing hours inquiries, and more;  
**清华大学新清华学堂 (New Tsinghua Xuetang)**: Provides ticketing services for performances and movies at New Tsinghua Xuetang (新清华学堂) and the Grand Auditorium (大礼堂);  
**清华大学艺术博物馆 (THU Art Museum)**: Provides ticketing services for the Art Museum;  
**清华大学科学博物馆 (THU Science Museum)**: Provides information and services for the Science Museum;
