# English listening and speaking communication
#### Grima John Paul Christopher
1. Each class has a corresponding theme (such as Happiness, Art, etc.), and the course content is supplemented by how to take notes
2. There is basically no listening and speaking part in the after-school homework, and you will be asked to write a short essay of 700 to 800 words.
3. Speaking exams, as well as group assignments, are related to the course topics
4. Being kind and answering questions in class will encourage students who are afraid to speak up.
