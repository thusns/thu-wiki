# English for Science and Technology Audio-visual and Listening (Intermediate, Advanced)



Choose this course carefully. The assignments are very complex. There are generally more than ten weekly assignments. There is also a saying of "fail one part, then fail the course." The teacher is intolerant of dropping out of the course mid-term and assigns a large number of vacation preview homework and regular homework. It is expected that the time spent on this course will be more than an hour every day.

This course is highly recommended to students who need to improve their English. Today, as English courses at Tsinghua University are becoming more and more popular, Teacher Wang Ying strictly requires students from all aspects, including comprehensive and scientific vocabulary and English proficiency. Fully understanding the multiple meanings of each word in context requires a lot of memorization and word work, and becoming proficient in listening to English conversations also requires a lot of listening training, so it is not surprising that this course has a lot of homework.

Teacher Wang Ying also offers a course called "The Power of Vocabulary". It is said that people who take the teacher's three courses can have a vocabulary level of up to 8,000. . .
