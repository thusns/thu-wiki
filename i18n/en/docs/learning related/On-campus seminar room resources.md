# On-campus seminar room resources

The following seminar rooms can be reserved on the Tsinghua Home Network official account; when using, you need to hold a three-person student ID card and change the key at the teaching building administrator's office:
* Tsinghua Academy Seminar Room: There are 4 seminar rooms with 6-8 seats.
* Four Teaching Seminar Rooms: There are 12 seminar rooms with 6-10 seats.
* Six Teaching Seminar Rooms: There are 3 6-seater seminar rooms.
* Fatu Underground Seminar Room: There are 2 6-seater seminar rooms.

The following seminar rooms can be reserved at Student Tsinghua (website, official account, mini program):
* 2nd floor of Building C: There are three 10-person seminar rooms and 4 20-person seminar rooms.
* The third floor of Building C: has 6 small seminar rooms (2 people), 4 medium-sized seminar rooms (11 people), and 2 large seminar rooms (about 30 people);
* South Area Underground: There are 12 research rooms ranging from 10 to 200 people.
