# On-campus coffee shop

(To be added, taste, price...)

### Qingqing coffee

South side of Guanchou Garden, Haidian District

1. Drinks cost 20-30 per person, and meals cost 50-70 per person.
2. The taste is okay, the portion is large
3. The decoration is simple and high-end, the environment is elegant and quiet (although it is very popular), and the WiFi is not very powerful

### Shi Nian Coffee

Southwest corner of Xinxuetang intersection (near Meng Minwei Concert Hall) ------Business hours: 8:00-22:00

1. Prices are generally expensive
2. The serving speed is slow, (the American coffee is thunderous, the red velvet and poplar nectar are planted?)
3. European style decoration, with terrace

### Anjia Chef

Peking University Yandongyuan Store------Business hours: 9:00-17:00

1. The price is normal (some are more expensive, but some are affordable, specifically a pineapple bun is 7 yuan)
2. The dishes are highly praised
3. The environment is relatively noisy and there are many people (QAQ sometimes has to queue up to buy bread)

### Youlika

At the end of the corridor on the second floor of Zhilan Garden (No. 7, Heqing Road, near the Tsinghua International Student Affairs Office); occasionally cats will appear; it is open until early in the morning, and you can purchase a night-time package to enjoy the night.

### Dufeng Academy

About 60 meters directly in front of Guanchou Garden, it is on the second floor (go up via the open-air ladder) ------Business hours: 9:30-21:30 (Is it still open now?)

1. The price is relatively affordable, and there are flash sales from time to time that are almost free of charge.
2. The food is generally well received (the burger is huge)
3. The environment is great, old style, with bookshelves. There is a terrace. Suitable for 1-5 people.

### Qingqing Yonghe

The first floor of Guanchou Garden (northwest) ------Business hours: 9:00-21:00

Requires entry from outside;

1. The price-performance ratio is average, but the price is on the expensive side. A meal costs RMB 60-100 per person, and the set meal is cheaper.
2. The taste is OK, but the serving speed is slow (30min)
3. The environment is relatively good, there are generally few people, and crowds are triggered randomly
