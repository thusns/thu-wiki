### Universal Beijing Resort

Distance: **34.8km**
Opening hours: 10:00-19:00 (last admission at 18:00)
Ticket price: 528￥
Introduction: The third Universal Studios theme park in Asia and the fifth in the world, it attracts countless young people who love theme parks.
### Shijingshan Amusement Park
Distance: **14.9**
Opening hours: 9:00-16:30
Ticket price: ￥5 for students, extra charges include items in the park
Introduction: The earliest amusement park built in Beijing, the Ferris wheel inside is the childhood memory of countless people.
### Beijing Happy Valley

Distance: **21.3km**
Opening hours: 9:30-22:00
Ticket price: Under 18 years old [258￥], under 1.5 meters tall [193￥], adults 297￥
Introduction: A theme park suitable for the whole family, where you can enjoy the beautiful scenery, watch performances, and play a lot of exciting and interesting rides.
