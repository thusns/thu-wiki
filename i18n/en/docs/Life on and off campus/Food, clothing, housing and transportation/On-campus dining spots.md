# On-campus dining spots
1. Taoli Casual Restaurant: Located in B1 of Taoli Garden, there are many tables for 4 people and can also accommodate more than 4 people;
2. Bauhinia Qingqing Pizza: Located in Bauhinia Garden B1, it can provide a large number of people for gatherings;
3. Taoli Grilled Fish: Located on the 3rd floor of Taoli Garden, with large tables, **not open yet**;
4. Floor 3 of Guanchou Garden: There are large tables. Reservations are required during peak periods. For reservations, call 62782563;
5. Level 1, Yushu Garden: Private rooms are available for reservation, please call 62794076 for reservation;
6. Qingqing Cafe: Provides Western food;
7. Zhilan Garden
8. Xichun Garden: reservation phone number 62783074;
