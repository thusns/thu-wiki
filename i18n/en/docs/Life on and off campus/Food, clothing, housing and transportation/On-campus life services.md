# On-campus supermarket

## Tmall Supermarket (Building 7, South District) (next to Qingfen Garden)

Downstairs, No. 7, southeast corner of Qingfenyuan, business hours: 8:30 – 23:30

* You can buy snacks, stationery and various daily necessities.
* There are also shops selling pancakes, octopus balls, and grilled cold noodles.
* The store also has a microwave, seating and charging sockets.
* You can pay with campus card or Alipay without checkout. There are also print shops, Seven Harbors and Nine Milk Tea Shops, laundromats (said to be able to repair suitcases), and electronic product stores (selling charging cables, network cables, etc.)

## Tmall Supermarket (C Building Store)

B1 of Building C, business hours: 8:30 – 23:30

* The variety is complete and the scale is large, and Alipay can be used for unmanned checkout payment.
* Various products, including snack bars and happy lemons, can be purchased through the mobile Taobao app - Taoxianda" and delivered to your door (delivery fee is free for purchases over 39 yuan).
* Likewise, there's a microwave, seating, and charging sockets.
* There is an ==Optical Shop== on the basement floor of Building C (but if you are looking for cost-effectiveness, it is recommended to go to Zhongguancun off campus) and ==Bookstore==.

## Tmall Supermarket (Guanchouyuan Store) (10,000 people underground)

G/F, Guanchou Garden, business hours: 9:00 – 21:00
(Opening hours during the epidemic are 10:00-19:00)

* The product types, in-store facilities and payment methods are similar to the previous two, but the basement level of Guanchou Garden also offers a variety of products, including gifts, cultural and educational supplies, fruits, etc.
* The ==Digital Repair Shop== on the basement floor can repair mobile phone screens.
* There is also an ==Optical Shop== next to Dufeng Bookstore not far from Guanchou Garden.
* There is a ==suit shop== on the ground floor of Wanwan (Guanchou Garden) where you can borrow suits.

## Tsinghua Lanyuan Community Business Center

Inside Zhaolan Hospital, business hours: 9:00 – 20:30
(Opening hours during the epidemic are 10:00-20:00)

* There are supermarkets, vegetable markets, fruit shops, flower shops, watch shops, etc. in Zhaolan courtyard.
* The prices in the supermarket are relatively reasonable and the products are complete in variety.
