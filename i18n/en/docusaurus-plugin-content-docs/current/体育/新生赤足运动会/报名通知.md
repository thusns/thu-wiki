# Freshman "Health Starts from the Ground Up" (健康始于足下) — Barefoot Sports Meet Registration Notice

## Purpose

To comprehensively implement the central government's directives and the spirit of General Secretary Xi Jinping's important speeches, fully implement the Party's education policy, achieve the goals of the "National Fitness Plan (2021–2025)," and promote school sports that improve students' physical health, athletic skills, healthy physique, team awareness, and competitive spirit. On campus, this event provides a platform for physical activity and social exchange for freshmen, fully tapping into and mobilizing students' enthusiasm for physical exercise. To this end, the 2022 Freshman "Health Starts from the Ground Up" (健康始于足下) Barefoot Sports Meet is hereby organized.

## Date and Venue

### Competition Date

October 8, 2022 (Saturday); Advance events on October 6 (Thursday) afternoon; Tug of War on October 4–5 (Tuesday and Wednesday, noon and evening)

### Competition Venue

East side of the Comprehensive Gymnasium (综体东侧)

## Competition Events

### Barefoot Events

1. Individual: 100m, 60m Jump Rope Run, 100m Two-Person Three-Legged Race, Medicine Ball Throw, Long Jump, Standing Long Jump, 1-minute Jump Rope (single under).
2. Team: 10×60m Face-to-Face Relay (2 Female + 8 Male), 4×100m Relay (3 Male + 1 Female).

### Non-barefoot Events

Tug of War (1 class team per department, 16 Male + 2 Female), 3-minute Group Long Rope (including rope turners, 10 per team, any gender), 3000m (Men), 1500m (Women), 800m, 2-minute Shuttlecock Kicking, Pull-ups (Men).

> Before registration, each department must hold an intra-departmental selection competition; the champion class will represent the department in the university Freshman Tug of War.

## Eligibility and Registration

1. Teams are formed on a class basis. All officially registered 2022-cohort freshmen in good health may register.
2. 2022-cohort sports-specialized class students may register and compete; placements and prizes will be awarded based on results (scores will not count toward the Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) team total). However, "sports specialist" must be noted after their name when registering, e.g., *** (Sports Specialist).
3. Individual events: Each event is limited to 1 male and 1 female entry per class; each person may enter only 1 individual event but may also enter relay, tug of war, and 3-minute group long rope.
4. Team events: Relay and 3-minute group long rope (1 team per class); tug of war (champion class only).
5. If a class has too few female (or male) students to fill the required events, registration adjustments may be made as follows:
   Departmental sports coordinators should fill out Appendix 1 "2022 XX Department XX Class Roster" and Appendix 2 "2022 Freshman Sports Meet Registration Record Form," have them stamped by the department, and submit them to the Air Dome front desk (to be forwarded to Teacher Jiang) no later than noon on September 28 (Wednesday) for student identity verification.
   Departments may apply for adjustments only under the following conditions:
   1. If a class has fewer female students than event entries, one person may enter multiple events. Example: A department with 6 female students and 10 events — all 6 females each enter 1 event to fill 6 slots, and the remaining 4 events may be filled by 1 or more females entering additional events.
   2. If a class has fewer male students than event entries, one person may enter multiple events. Example: Humanities department with 6 male students and 10 events — all 6 males each enter 1 event to fill 6 slots, and the remaining 4 events may be filled by 1 or more males entering additional events.
   3. If a class has only 1 female or male student, that person may enter multiple events without restriction based on their physical ability and specialties. Please exercise good judgment based on personal fitness.
6. Registration Requirements
   1. Registration period: September 23, 2022 (Thursday) through September 28, 2022 (Wednesday) 24:00.
   2. Departments may register through the Shenghuo Competition Platform at: http://tsinghua.bjsh98.com.
   3. September 30, 2022 (Friday) 12:30–13:15, tug of war bracket draw at the conference room on the 2nd floor of the East Sports Field West Stands.

## Scoring and Placement

1. Top 8 in each event. Scores: 9, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. Team and relay event scores are doubled.
2. Top 12 in the freshman team total score will receive awards.
3. If team total scores are tied, the team with more individual event 1st-place finishes ranks higher; if still tied, the team with more 2nd-place finishes ranks higher, and so on.
4. Rankings based on each department's sports meet scores are counted toward the 2022–2023 academic year Ma Yuehan Cup (马约翰杯) total score.
5. Individuals in the top 3 of individual events and teams in the top 12 will receive material prizes.

## Competition Rules and Requirements

1. Barefoot events must be performed barefoot.
2. Participants should carry their student ID or other identity credentials for verification.
3. Cheating will result in disqualification of the entire unit's results and public criticism.
4. Specific requirements will be explained at the Team Leaders' Meeting.
   1. Team Leaders' Meeting: October 3, 2022 (Monday) 20:00.
   2. The meeting will be held online via "Tencent Meeting." The Tencent Meeting account number will be announced in the sports coordinator group chat.
