# On-campus travel

## school bus

* The campus bus is "waving to get on and get off at the nearest location", and you can carry large luggage.
  * You can use the "Tsinghua Campus Bus" WeChat applet to monitor the departure status of campus bus lines.
  * Those on campus can ride for free with their Tsinghua ID card, while those outside the school can ride for 2 yuan/person (3 yuan/person on weekends and holidays).
* Outer ring: 7:20-18:00 on weekdays, 8:30-17:30 on Saturdays, Sundays and statutory holidays. Operations during winter and summer vacations are subject to announcements.
* Inner ring: 7:40-11:10 am on weekdays and 12:40-17:25 pm, closed on Saturdays, Sundays and statutory holidays, closed during winter and summer vacations
* Student hotline: 7:40am, 9:20am, 1:00pm, 2:50pm on weekdays, closed on Saturdays, Sundays and statutory holidays, and closed during winter and summer vacations
* Special medical line: It goes to Peking University Third Hospital on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, and goes to Xiyuan Hospital on Tuesdays and Thursdays. It leaves the school hospital at 8:00 and stops at the Senior Activity Center at 8:05.

**_For details, please see "About Tsinghua" - "Practical Information" - "On-campus Transportation" in "Tsinghua University Official Website"_**
