# zoo

### Beijing Zoo

Distance: **7.6km**
Opening hours: 7:30-19:30 (latest admission 19:00)
Ticket price: 7.5￥ for students
Introduction: It has a history of 100 years. It is the earliest zoo in China and has the largest number of animals. Adults and children love to go there.
### Beijing Wildlife Park
Distance: **57.1km**
Opening hours: 9:00-17:00
Ticket price: 85￥ for students
Introduction: The animals here are not divided by family, genus or region. Children can participate, experience, interact and entertain.
### National Botanical Garden North Garden
Distance: **10.2km**
Opening hours: 6:00-20:00
Ticket price: students￥2.5
Introduction: There are many kinds of plants. The main visiting point is the greenhouse exhibition area, as well as many scenic spots and historic sites. It is a good place for recreation and leisure.
