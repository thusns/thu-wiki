For specific questions, it is recommended to ask the academic affairs department of the department to obtain professional answers.

## General requirements

Unless there are special reasons, undergraduate students are required to complete the "Writing and Communication" course in their freshman year.

The English course is compulsory with 4 credits, and you can choose courses according to the requirements of the English placement test; there is a limit of 4 credits, and you can choose courses independently.
