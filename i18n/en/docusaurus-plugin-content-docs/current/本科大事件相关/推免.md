## Related websites

1. Tsinghua University Information Portal

http://info.tsinghua.edu.cn/

Before the start of the promotion and exemption work, the Office of Academic Affairs will publish a notice on the promotion of examination exemption and postgraduate studies in the [Academic Affairs Announcement] column on the home page, including the conditions that students should meet when applying for exemption, registration time, operation manual of the exemption registration system, qualification review requirements, etc.

2. Tsinghua University Research and Recruitment Website

https://yz.tsinghua.edu.cn/ The Tsinghua University Research and Recruitment Platform will issue a notice of registration for graduate studies without examination. The assessment exemption and admission procedures for each department can be viewed through the Tsinghua University Graduate Admissions website, or you can go to the home page of each department. The time is subject to the release of each department.

3. Tsinghua University graduate student application service system

(Log in through the personal account of the old version of the info information portal - Comprehensive - Left quick navigation - Graduation column - Exam-free promotion, or log in through the new version of the info information portal - My Workbench - Exam-free promotion, or directly access the Tsinghua University graduate student application service system through the following website: https://yzbm.tsinghua.edu.cn/ndLogin, register and log in as required)

All undergraduate students of our school (including students studying at our school and graduate students outside the school) who participate in the examination-free recommendation to study for doctoral/master's degrees on or off campus that year should log in to the Tsinghua University graduate application service system within the time specified by the school and fill in the application information for the examination-free recommendation, otherwise it will be invalid. Students can use this system to check information such as whether they have obtained a recommended quota (maintained by undergraduate departments) and whether they have been admitted to the school (maintained by graduate admissions departments).

4. Nationally recommended information disclosure and management service system for outstanding fresh undergraduate graduates to study for graduate studies without examination

http://yz.chsi.com.cn/tm

This website is the recommendation and exemption service system of the Ministry of Education. It is a unified information filing and public platform and online application and admission system for the national recommendation and exemption work. Relevant work such as qualification review and confirmation, application, admission, and filing and disclosure of recommended students (including recommended master's students and direct doctoral students) must be carried out through this system. All exempted students who have obtained the quota and are accepted must log in to the system and perform corresponding operations in accordance with the requirements of the Ministry of Education.

## Exemption requirements

1. Obtain "recommendation qualifications" and "recommendation quotas" from our school.

2. Obtain an “admission quota” from the scientific research institution you wish to attend.
